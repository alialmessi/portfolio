Venture capital

From 500+ projects to high-conviction investments

At Wallex, one of Iran’s largest crypto exchanges (5M+ users), I worked as a VC business analyst in the research and investment team that deployed the company’s proprietary capital. My job was to turn a noisy, fast-moving market into investable theses.

Company
Wallex
Role
VC Business Analyst
Timeline
2023 — 2025
Focus
Blockchain · DeFi · Web3

The challenge

Thousands of tokens, a new narrative every week, and extreme volatility. How do you decide where proprietary capital should go, and protect it when the market turns?

The team needed a repeatable way to cover the whole market, separate durable protocols from hype, and turn research into allocation decisions that senior leadership could act on.

Screening funnel

Every protocol went through three layers of fundamental analysis. Only the ones that passed all three reached allocation.

Exhibit 1 · Three-layer screening funnel

500+ protocols screenedInfrastructure · Financial applications · Emerging sectors
1 · Protocol designTechnical viability, security, architecture
2 · TokenomicsInflation schedule, utility, incentive alignment
3 · Market positioningMoats, TVL, user adoption
High-conviction allocation

Portfolio strategies

Theses fed three complementary strategies, each with its own role in the portfolio.

Exhibit 2 · Portfolio strategies

Liquid venture

Asymmetric early upside

Early-stage liquidity and backing for new protocols where the potential upside far outweighs the risk.

Value / growth

Scaling with adoption

Increasing exposure as a protocol’s network effects, usage and utility grow.

DeFi yield

Capital efficiency

Harvesting AMM (automated market maker) and lending yields inside decentralized ecosystems.

Risk management

In crypto, returns mean little without downside control. The portfolio used a layered risk-management toolkit.

Exhibit 3 · Risk-management toolkit

01

Options & swaps

Derivatives for downside protection.

02

Staking

Baseline yield on core holdings.

03

Liquidity provision

Fee capture to offset drawdowns.

04

Delta-neutral

Perpetual futures to isolate yield from price moves.

My role

  • Fundamental analysis and due diligence across 500+ blockchain, DeFi and Web3 protocols, turned into investment theses and recommendations.
  • Supported asset allocation and portfolio construction across the three strategies.
  • Market monitoring: narratives, macro indicators, tokenomics, sentiment and on-chain data, feeding portfolio decisions and senior reporting.

Results

A portfolio that grew strongly while keeping volatility under control.
