Designing a fintech proposition for Iran’s mass-affluent

Iran’s mass-affluent have financial needs that current services don’t meet well. On this consulting project I’m helping define what a new fintech should offer them, and how it should enter the market.

Client
Mohajer
Role
Business Analyst
Timeline
2026 — Present
Stage
Product discovery

The question

What should a fintech built for the mass-affluent actually offer, and where is the best entry point?

This segment sits between mass retail banking and private wealth management, and neither serves it well. The challenge is finding a proposition that is clearly needed and that can be built.

Approach

Learn from four adjacent markets, then combine the findings into clear options.

My contribution

  • Product discovery and market research on the financial needs of Iran’s mass-affluent segment.
  • Benchmark research across wealth management, global mobility, digital assets and cross-border payments to find relevant business models.
  • User archetypes, opportunity maps, concept directions and market-entry options to support product definition.

Where it stands

The work is framing the next phase of decisions on product definition.

The project is ongoing. The outputs give the team a shared picture of the customer, a map of where to play, and concrete options for how to enter the market.
